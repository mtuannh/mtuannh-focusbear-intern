## What are common cyber security threats in a remote work environment?

Some common threats in remote work include phishing emails, suspicious links, weak passwords, stolen login details, and using unsecured devices or networks. Since people may work outside the office, it is important to be careful about what links they open and where they access company accounts.

## What are best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?

I should use strong and unique passwords for my accounts and enable 2FA where possible. I should also keep my computer and phone updated and avoid installing software or opening files from unknown sources. When working remotely, I should also be careful when using public or unsecured networks.

## Why is it important to lock your computer when away from your desk?

Locking my computer prevents other people from accessing my accounts or information when I am away from my desk. This is especially important when working with company or user information because someone could access it without my permission if my computer is left unlocked.

## How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?

I should not click on suspicious links or open unexpected attachments. I should check who sent the message and look carefully at the link or email address before taking any action. If I am unsure whether something is legitimate, I should ask someone in the team instead of taking the risk.

## What makes a strong password and why should you use a password manager?

A strong password should be difficult to guess and should not be reused across different accounts. Using different passwords means that if one account is compromised, the other accounts are less likely to be affected.

A password manager can help store and manage different strong passwords without needing to remember all of them individually.

## Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important, and when should you enable it?

2FA adds another layer of security because logging in requires more than just a password. Even if someone gets my password, they may still not be able to access the account without the second verification method. I should enable 2FA for work accounts and other important accounts whenever it is available.

## What security measures do you currently follow, and where can you improve?

I already try to be careful about suspicious links and avoid sharing my passwords with other people. I also use a laptop stand and external monitor for my workspace, but for security I need to make sure that I consistently lock my computer whenever I step away.

I can also improve by making 2FA and strong, unique passwords a normal part of how I manage my accounts instead of only thinking about security when there is a problem.

## How can you make secure behaviour a habit rather than an afterthought?

I can make security part of my normal workflow by checking links before opening them, locking my computer when I leave my desk, and checking what information I am sharing before sending it. Doing these things regularly will make them become normal habits instead of something I only remember after a security issue happens.

## What steps will you take to ensure your passwords and accounts are secure?

I will use strong and unique passwords for my work accounts and enable 2FA where possible. I will avoid sharing my passwords and will not store them in insecure places. If I use a password manager, I will make sure the password manager itself is protected properly.

## What would you do if you suspected a security breach or suspicious activity on your account?

If I noticed suspicious activity, I would avoid making further changes that could make the situation worse and report it to my supervisor or the appropriate person in the team. I would also follow the company's security process and provide the relevant information about what happened.

One habit I will follow during my internship is to **lock my computer whenever I leave my desk, even for a short period of time**. This is a simple step, but it prevents other people from accessing my work or company information while I am away.
