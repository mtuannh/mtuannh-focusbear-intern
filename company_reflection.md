## Why do I think Focus Bear was created?

I think Focus Bear was created because the founders experienced the problem themselves. From what I learned from the website and also joined my onboarding session, the team tried different productivity and wellness apps but found that they did not really fit the way their brains worked. Instead of creating another normal productivity app, they decided to build something that could actually help people stay focused and build better habits.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

The main problem Focus Bear is trying to solve is distraction and difficulty maintaining focus. From my perspective, it is very easy to get distracted by social media, websites, notifications, emails and other things while studying or working. Even when someone knows they should be focusing, it can still be difficult to stop themselves from checking something. So Focus Bear tries to solve this by reducing the number of distractions around the user. It can block distracting websites and apps, guide users through morning and evening routines, and remind them to take breaks.

## Why do I think this mission is important?

I think this mission is important because being distracted is becoming a normal part of how we use technology. Many apps are designed to keep people coming back and checking them, which can make it harder to stay focused on something that actually matters. I also like that Focus Bear focuses on building habits rather than just expecting people to have enough self-control. So basically, this makes the problem more realistic.

## Do I personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

Yes, I can relate. As a university student, there have definitely been times when I planned to study or work on an assignment but ended up spending more time on my phone, social media or other distractions. Sometimes the difficult part is not knowing what I need to do, but actually staying focused long enough to finish it. This is one of the reasons I find Focus Bear interesting.
