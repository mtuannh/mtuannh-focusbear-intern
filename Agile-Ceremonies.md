# Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration

## Research & Learn

### What are the main Agile ceremonies, and what is their purpose?

#### Daily Stand-ups

Daily stand-ups are short meetings where team members share what they worked on, what they are currently working on, and whether they have any blockers. The main purpose is to keep everyone updated and identify problems early.

#### Sprint Planning vs. Continuous Prioritization

Sprint Planning is commonly used in Scrum to decide what work the team will focus on during the upcoming sprint. The team reviews the backlog, discusses priorities and agrees on the work that can realistically be completed.

In Kanban, work is usually prioritised continuously instead of being planned around fixed sprints. New tasks can be added and prioritised as the team's capacity changes. This makes Kanban more flexible when priorities change frequently.

#### Retrospectives

A retrospective is a meeting where the team looks back at their recent work and discusses what went well, what could have been improved, and what they can do differently next time. The purpose is to continuously improve the team's workflow and collaboration.

#### Backlog Refinement

Backlog refinement is the process of reviewing and organising upcoming tasks. The team discusses requirements, clarifies unclear tasks, estimates the work where necessary, and makes sure the highest-priority tasks are ready to be worked on.

### How do Agile teams collaborate asynchronously and across time zones?

Agile teams can use tools such as Slack, GitHub, project boards and documentation to communicate without everyone needing to be online at the same time. Team members can leave clear updates, comments and documentation so that others can understand the current progress when they are available.

Having clear task statuses and written updates is especially important when working across different time zones because it reduces the need for constant meetings and allows team members to continue their work without waiting for an immediate response.

## Reflection

### How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?

Agile ceremonies help the team stay aligned by creating regular opportunities to share progress, discuss problems and clarify priorities. For example, stand-ups make it easier to understand what everyone is working on, while retrospectives give the team a chance to discuss problems and improve the way they work.

I also think these ceremonies are useful for an intern because they provide a better understanding of what the team is currently focusing on and make it easier to ask questions when I am unsure about something.

### Which ceremony do you think is most important for your role, and why?

I think daily stand-ups are the most important ceremony for my role as an intern. Since I am still learning the project and becoming familiar with the codebase, the stand-up gives me a good opportunity to understand what the team is working on and communicate my own progress.

It also gives me a chance to mention blockers or ask for clarification before spending too much time going in the wrong direction.
