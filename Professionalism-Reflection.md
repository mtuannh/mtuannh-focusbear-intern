## Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?

In university group projects, I have experienced that teamwork works better when everyone communicates clearly and takes responsibility for their own work. I think a team becomes more effective when people are comfortable sharing their ideas, asking questions, and helping each other when someone is stuck. I also think being respectful and keeping everyone updated are important parts of good teamwork.

## What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?

I will try to keep my messages clear and get straight to the point, especially when discussing tasks or problems. If I am not sure about something, I will ask questions instead of making assumptions. I will also listen to other people's opinions and make sure I communicate in a respectful way, even when I have a different opinion.

## How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?

I can contribute by being friendly and respectful to my teammates while still taking my work seriously. I think it is important to be comfortable having casual conversations and having fun with the team, but I also need to know when to focus on work. I can also help create a positive environment by being willing to help others and keeping a positive attitude when facing problems.

## How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?

Before a meeting, I can review what I have been working on and make sure I understand my current progress. I should also think about any questions or blockers I need to discuss so I do not forget them during the meeting. During the meeting, I will listen carefully and take note of any important feedback or actions I need to follow up on.

## What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?

Being proactive means not always waiting for someone to tell me what to do. For example, if I am unsure about a task, I can ask for clarification instead of staying stuck for too long. I can also keep my team updated about my progress and raise blockers early. If I finish a task, I can check what needs to be done next or ask my supervisor for the next priority.

## How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?

If I need information from a teammate or supervisor, I will first send a clear and polite message explaining what I need and why I need it. If I do not get a response, I can follow up after giving them reasonable time, especially if the issue is blocking my work. If the issue continues to affect my task and I cannot make progress, I would raise it during a stand-up or discuss it with my supervisor rather than repeatedly sending messages.

## What are some things you won't do?

I will not ignore blockers or stay silent when I do not understand something. I will not blame other people when something goes wrong, and I will not respond defensively to feedback. I also will not make assumptions about other people's intentions or communicate in a disrespectful way. If I make a mistake, I will take responsibility for it and focus on how I can fix it and avoid making the same mistake again.

From this task, I understand that professionalism is not only about how I do my technical work. It is also about how I communicate, respond to feedback, take responsibility, and work with other people. Since Focus Bear has a relaxed and friendly work environment, I think it is important for me to enjoy working with the team while still being responsible and respectful.
