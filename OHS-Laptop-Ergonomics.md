## What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?

Using a laptop for a long time without an external monitor or keyboard can cause poor posture. The screen is usually lower than eye level, so I may need to look down for a long time. This can cause neck and shoulder strain. Using the built-in keyboard for a long time can also make my wrists and hands uncomfortable, especially if my laptop is not positioned properly.

## What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop?

A laptop stand can raise the screen closer to eye level and help me sit in a better position. An external keyboard and mouse can then be used at a more comfortable height instead of reaching up to the laptop. An ergonomic chair can also provide better support for my back and help me maintain a better sitting position.

## What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup?

The screen should be positioned around eye level so I do not need to constantly look down. My chair should allow my feet to rest comfortably on the floor and support my back. I should also keep the keyboard and mouse at a comfortable height so my arms and wrists are relaxed while working.

## What are some daily habits that reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use?

I should take regular movement breaks instead of sitting in the same position for a long time. I can also check my posture throughout the day and adjust my sitting position when needed. Taking short breaks from looking at the screen can also help reduce eye strain.

## What equipment changes can you make to improve your workspace setup?

One useful change would be using a laptop stand to raise the screen to a more comfortable height. If available, I can also use an external keyboard and mouse so that I do not need to keep my hands and wrists in an uncomfortable position.

## What behavioural changes can you implement to improve posture and reduce strain?

I can make a habit of checking my posture during the day and avoiding sitting in the same position for too long. I can also take regular movement breaks, stretch, and adjust my screen or chair when I notice that I am sitting uncomfortably.
