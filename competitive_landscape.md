## What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?

After looking at Freedom, Cold Turkey, Tiimo and Routinery, I think Focus Bear is different because it combines **distraction blocking with routines and breaks** instead of focusing on only one area.

Freedom and Cold Turkey are mainly focused on blocking distracting websites and apps. Freedom also has features such as scheduling and Locked Mode, while Cold Turkey provides detailed website blocking options. Tiimo is more focused on visual planning, task management and flexible scheduling, and Routinery focuses heavily on guiding users through routines with timers and reminders.

Focus Bear combines some of these ideas in one product. It can block distractions across devices, guide users through morning and evening routines, and provide breaks during focused work. I also think its focus on ADHD and neurodivergent users gives the product a clearer target audience.

## If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?

If I wanted one app to help me both **stay focused and build better routines**, I would choose Focus Bear because I would not need to use a separate app for blocking distractions and another one for routines.

I also like the fact that Focus Bear can connect focus sessions across my computer and phone. For example, when I start a focus session on my computer, my phone can also enter a less distracting mode. This would be useful because switching from a blocked computer to my phone could otherwise be an easy way to get distracted.

However, I would not say Focus Bear is the best choice for everyone. If I mainly wanted a visual planner, I might prefer Tiimo. If my main goal was strong website and app blocking, Freedom or Cold Turkey could also be better choices.

## What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?

One feature I noticed in Tiimo that I did not see listed in Focus Bear's current features is **mood tracking**. Tiimo allows users to record how they feel and use this information to reflect on their focus and energy over time.

I think this could be useful for Focus Bear because users could compare their mood or energy with their routines and focus sessions. It could help users understand which habits or routines work best for them.

## Based on your research, what's one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?

One improvement I think Focus Bear could make is adding a simple **daily mood and energy check-in**. It does not need to be complicated. Users could quickly record how they feel before or after completing their routines or focus sessions.

This could make the progress tracking more useful because users would be able to look at both their productivity and how they felt during the day. It could also help users identify patterns and understand which routines are actually helping them.
