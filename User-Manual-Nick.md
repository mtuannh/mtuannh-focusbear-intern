# User Manual for Nick Nguyen

## My role

- I am working on:
  - Frontend development tasks.
  - Learning and working with Focus Bear's existing codebase.
  - Onboarding and development tasks assigned by the team.
- The days and times I work are:
  - Mondays (9am - 5pm).
  - I also attend daily stand-ups throughout the internship (not all the meetings).

## How I Work Best

- I do my best work when:
  - I understand what the task is and what the expected outcome is.
  - I have clear requirements or examples to follow.
  - I can focus on one task at a time.
  - I have enough time to understand the code before making changes.
- I struggle when:
  - The requirements are unclear.
  - I do not know what I should work on next.
  - I have to make assumptions about what is expected.

## Communication Style

- I prefer messages to be:
  - Clear and direct.
  - Short when the question is simple.
  - Detailed when the task or problem is more complicated.
  - With enough context for me to understand what is needed.
- The best way to reach me is:
  - Discord/Teams for normal work communication.
  - Daily stand-ups for progress updates and blockers.
  - Direct messages when I need to clarify something about my task.
- If you need something from me, please:
  - Tell me clearly what you need and when it is needed.
  - If there is a specific requirement, an example is helpful.

## Collaboration & Teamwork

- I work best in a team when:
  - Everyone communicates clearly about their progress.
  - People are comfortable asking questions and sharing ideas.
  - Everyone takes responsibility for their own work.
  - We can ask for help when we are stuck.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is:
  - Unclear communication that causes unnecessary delays.
  - Not knowing what needs to be done next.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means:
  - I am listening and trying to understand the discussion.
  - I may need some time to process the information before responding.

## Focus & Productivity

- I stay focused by:
  - Working on one task at a time.
  - Breaking a larger task into smaller steps.
  - Keeping track of what I need to complete.
- Things that distract me:
  - Unclear tasks.
  - Too many things to work on at the same time.
  - Having to switch between different tasks too often.
- The best way to support my productivity is:
  - Give me a clear task and expected outcome.
  - Let me know what the priority is.
  - Give me feedback when something needs to be changed.

## Feedback & Growth

- I like feedback to be:
  - Clear and direct.
  - Specific, so I understand what needs to be improved.
  - Focused on the work rather than the person.
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is:
  - Tell me directly what went wrong and how I can improve it.
- I appreciate it when teammates:
  - Explain why something should be changed.
  - Share better approaches or examples.
  - Give me the chance to ask questions and learn from the feedback.

## Fun Facts & Quirks

- One thing people might misunderstand about me is:
  - If I ask a lot of questions, it is usually because I want to make sure I understand the task properly, not because I am trying to overcomplicate it.
- A fun fact about me:
  - I am interested in technology and have been exploring different areas of software development, especially frontend development and data science.
- My work superpower is:
  - I am willing to learn new things and keep trying when I do not understand something at first.
