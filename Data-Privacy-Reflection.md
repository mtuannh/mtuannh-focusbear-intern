I should only access data that I need for my work and avoid sharing sensitive information with people who do not need it. I should also be careful when sending information through messages, emails, or other tools and make sure I am sharing it with the correct person.

Sensitive information should only be stored in approved systems and should not be kept in unnecessary personal files or locations. When sharing information, I should make sure the recipient is authorised to receive it and only share what is necessary.

If I no longer need sensitive information, I should remove it according to the company's process rather than keeping unnecessary copies. I should also avoid putting confidential information into source code, public repositories, or other places where it could be exposed.
